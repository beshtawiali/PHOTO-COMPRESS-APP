Privacy Policy for Photo Compressor

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Photo Compressor ("the app", "we", "our", or "us") accesses, uses, processes, and shares information when you use the Photo Compressor Android application.

Photo Compressor is provided by the developer of the Photo Compressor application listed on Google Play.

1. About Photo Compressor

Photo Compressor is an Android application that allows users to select, compress, resize, and save images on their devices.

2. Photos and Images

Photo Compressor may access photos or images that you choose in order to provide image compression and resizing functionality.

Images selected by you are processed for the purpose of performing the requested compression or resizing operation. Photo Compressor does not intentionally upload your personal photos to our own servers for storage.

Access to photos or media is used only when necessary to provide the app's image-related functionality.

The app uses Appodeal, a third-party advertising and mediation platform, to display and manage advertisements. Appodeal may work with multiple advertising networks and technology providers to deliver, measure, personalize, and improve advertisements.

Depending on the user's device, region, privacy choices, and applicable law, Appodeal and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available
  • Device identifiers and other identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device model and device type
  • Operating system and technical device information
  • Network and connection information
  • Advertising data
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and other interactions with advertisements
  • Product interaction information
  • Diagnostics, crash information, and app performance information
  • Approximate location where enabled or inferred from information such as IP address

This information may be used for purposes including providing advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud and abuse, analytics, diagnostics, and, where permitted by applicable law and the user's consent or privacy choices, personalized advertising.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Photo Compressor does not maintain user accounts and does not intentionally store users' selected personal photos on our own servers.

Information processed independently by third-party advertising providers may be retained according to their respective privacy policies, legal obligations, and retention practices.

Because Photo Compressor does not provide user accounts or maintain an independent server-side user profile for its core functionality, there is generally no Photo Compressor account or server-side photo archive that requires an account deletion process.
