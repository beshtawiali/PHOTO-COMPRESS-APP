Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. About Photo Compressor

Photo Compressor is an Android application that allows users to compress and resize images on their devices.

2. Photos and Files

The app may access photos or images selected by the user in order to provide image compression and resizing functionality.

Images selected for processing are used only to perform the features requested by the user. Photo Compressor does not intentionally upload or store users' personal photos on our own servers.
