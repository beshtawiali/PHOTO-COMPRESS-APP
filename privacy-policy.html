Privacy Policy for Photo Compressor

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Photo Compressor ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you use the Photo Compressor mobile application.

1. About Photo Compressor

Photo Compressor is an Android application that allows users to compress, resize, and optimize images on their devices.

2. Photos and Files

The app allows users to select photos or images in order to provide image compression, resizing, and related functionality.

Images selected by the user are processed locally on the user's device for the requested image-processing functionality. Photo Compressor does not intentionally upload or store users' personal photos on our own servers.

Advertising services and their partners may automatically collect or process certain information, which may include:
